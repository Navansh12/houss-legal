Delete your Houss account

Houss is operated by Gyan Prakash and Sons. You can request permanent deletion of your Houss account and associated personal data. We complete deletion within 60 days.

Website: www.houss.co.in

In the app (recommended)

  1. Open Houss and sign in
  2. Go to Profile
  3. Scroll to the bottom → tap Account & privacy (small link under Log out)
  4. Tap Delete my account
  5. Complete the three confirmation steps

You will be logged out immediately. You cannot cancel the request in the app.

By email

Email info@houss.co.in from a reachable address and include:

  • The mobile number registered on Houss
  • The words: Please delete my Houss account

Main owners must transfer ownership or remove their household before the in-app delete will proceed. Questions: info@houss.co.in
