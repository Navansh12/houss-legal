1. Active accounts

Data is retained while your account is active and needed to provide the Houss service.

2. Retention periods (draft)

Data type Retention
User profile (phone, name, DOB) Until account deletion / day-60 purge
OTP logs 24 hours (or ephemeral for closed-beta shared code)
Notifications Until user deletes or 90 days idle
Timeline / activity events Life of household + 1 year after household delete
Shopping / meal / driver records Life of household + 7 years (financial dispute buffer — counsel to confirm)
Attendance records (incl. location-verified flags) Life of household + period counsel confirms
Staff duty checklists & item completion ticks Life of household (or until list deleted); completions tied to checklist items
Voice audio Not retained — transcribed then discarded; no persistent voice recording store
Voice transcript Used in-session for preview/confirm; not stored as a dedicated voice history
Photos (gate, shopping, meal/driver refs, task status, vehicle/appliance, receipts) Life of household until file deleted or household purged
Staff documents (ID/compliance vault) Until staff removes, share revoked, or 1 year after employment link ends (counsel to confirm)
Payroll / advances / salary ledger entries Life of household + legal/tax retention (counsel to confirm)
Account deletion requests / ops logs As needed to complete purge + audit
Subscription / promo entitlement While relevant to access + billing records when payments live
Analytics events 24 months aggregated (when enabled)
Server logs 90 days
Backups 30 days rolling

3. Deletion & anonymization

On account deletion (see our [Account deletion](account-deletion.html) page):

  • Personal identifiers are removed or replaced with anonymized values
  • Device tokens, notifications, and that user’s staff documents are cleared
  • Phone number is released after the cooling / purge period
  • Household history for other members may be kept with the actor anonymized

4. Household deletion

When a master admin deletes a household (when enabled), household-scoped data is removed according to our database rules (cascade delete).

5. Legal holds

Data may be preserved when required by law, court order, or an active dispute.

6. Contact

Questions: [info@houss.co.in](mailto:info@houss.co.in)

We will update this policy when we add new data types or change how long we keep information.
