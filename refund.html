1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to paid subscriptions for Houss Plan A (Plus) and Plan B (Multi) when billing is active through Razorpay, Google Play, or the Apple App Store.

It does not apply during periods when Houss is in free testing, closed beta, or promo-only access with no charge.

2. Free plan

The Houss Free plan costs ₹0. No payment is taken and no refunds apply.

3. Closed beta / testing (current)

Razorpay is not live yet. Paid checkout is not enabled. Upgrades during beta are typically via promo / invite codes with no payment taken. This policy applies when public paid billing launches.

4. Paid plans — summary (when live)

Published India pricing (GST inclusive, when live):

Plan Monthly Annual
Plan A (Plus) ₹399 ₹3,990
Plan B (Multi) ₹699 ₹6,990

Founding offers and trials may apply as shown in the app or on www.houss.co.in at the time of purchase.

5. Free trial

When a 15-day Plan A trial is offered, you will not be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. If you do not cancel, billing may begin automatically according to the plan you selected.

6. Monthly subscriptions

  • Monthly plans renew each month until cancelled.
  • No refund is provided for partial months after a billing cycle has started.
  • Cancelling stops future renewals; you may retain access until the end of the current paid period, depending on platform rules.

7. Annual subscriptions

  • If you cancel an annual plan mid-year, no automatic pro-rata refund is guaranteed unless required by law or app store policy.
  • Exception (draft goodwill policy): Within 7 days of first annual purchase, you may request a full refund if you have not materially used paid-only features beyond basic setup. We decide case-by-case in line with payment processor rules.

8. Billing errors

If you were charged incorrectly (duplicate charge, wrong plan, technical error), contact us within 30 days for a full refund of the erroneous amount after verification.

9. App store purchases

If you subscribed through Google Play or the Apple App Store, refunds must often be requested through that store's refund process. We will assist where we can, but store policies and timelines apply.

10. How to request a refund

Email info@houss.co.in with:

  1. Subject line: "Houss refund request"
  2. Registered mobile number
  3. Date and amount of payment
  4. Payment reference or receipt (Razorpay / store order ID)
  5. Brief reason for the request

We aim to respond within 7 business days. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method where possible.

11. Chargebacks

Please contact us before initiating a chargeback so we can resolve the issue. Unfounded chargebacks may result in account suspension.

12. Changes

We may update this Refund Policy. The current version will be posted at www.houss.co.in with an updated date.
