Privacy Policy — Houss

Effective date: 25 August 2026

Product: Houss — Everything home. One app.

Website: https://www.houss.co.in (also https://houss.co.in)

Company / operator: Gyan Prakash and Sons

1. Introduction

Gyan Prakash and Sons ("we", "us", "our") provides the Houss mobile application for household coordination in India. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, who can see it, and your choices.

By using Houss, you agree to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Who this applies to

This policy applies to:

  • Household owners and family members who use Houss
  • Domestic staff (cooks, drivers, guards, and other helps) invited to a household
  • Child accounts added by a household owner
  • Visitors to our website at www.houss.co.in

Houss is intended for users 18 years and older. Child accounts are created and controlled by the household owner (master admin), not by children signing up independently.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Account and profile

Data Why we collect it
Mobile phone number Login (OTP), account identity
Name Display in the household
Date of birth (optional) Profile, birthdays on family calendar
Preferred language App display (where available)
Account deletion status Process delete requests

3.2 Household and property data

Data Why we collect it
Household name Identify your home in the app
Property names and addresses Meals, driver, shopping, and other features scoped per property
Role (owner, family, staff, child) Show the right screens and permissions
Permission settings Control what each person can see and do
Subscription / promo status Enforce plan limits

3.3 App activity and content you create

Depending on how you use Houss, we may process:

  • Meal requests and kitchen updates
  • Driver trip bookings, pickup/drop details, and map locations you pin
  • Shopping list items (including optional reference photos)
  • Attendance check-in and check-out (including GPS when self check-in is used)
  • Leave requests and approvals
  • Family calendar events and staff task reminders
  • Gate intake photos and notes (courier, parcel, letter, etc.)
  • Task status photos and notes on meals, trips, and shopping
  • Staff duty checklists and completion ticks
  • Voice commands (see §3.4)
  • Payroll-related amounts entered by the household (salary, advances, deductions)
  • Vehicles, appliances, expenses, and staff documents (where you use those features)
  • Activity timeline and in-app notifications

3.4 Voice commands

When you use Voice, you may speak or type a command. Spoken audio may be sent to our servers for transcription (and optionally to an AI provider such as OpenAI if configured). We do not keep the audio file after it is transcribed. The text is used to preview tasks; confirmed tasks become normal meal, driver, or shopping records.

3.5 Photos and files

If you or your staff use photo features, we store images you upload (gate intake, shopping references, meal/driver references, task completion, vehicle/appliance documents, staff ID/compliance documents, receipts). Photos are linked to your household and are not public.

3.6 Device and technical data

Data Why we collect it
Push notification token Send alerts about household activity
Device platform (iOS / Android) Deliver notifications correctly
App version and basic logs Security, debugging, and service reliability

We do not sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

3.7 Payments (when enabled)

Razorpay is not live yet. During closed beta, plan upgrades may use promo codes without charging real money. When paid plans are offered, payment processing may be handled by Razorpay or app store billing. We receive subscription status and billing references — not your full card or UPI credentials.

4. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  • Provide and operate Houss features
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure
  • Send push notifications and in-app alerts you expect
  • Enforce household permissions and subscription plan limits
  • Process account deletion
  • Improve reliability and fix problems
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable law

5. Who can see your information

Within your household: Data is visible according to role and permissions set by the master admin.

Staff working in multiple homes: Data is kept strictly separate between households.

Service providers: We may share limited data with trusted processors who help us run Houss, such as:

  • Cloud hosting (Supabase database in India ap-south-1; API on Render)
  • SMS/OTP delivery providers (real SMS planned; closed beta may use a shared test code)
  • Push notification services (e.g. Apple / Google / Expo)
  • Speech-to-text / language understanding for Voice (when configured)
  • Payment processors (when subscriptions are live)
  • Analytics providers (only if and when we enable analytics)

Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect rights, safety, and security.

6. Where data is stored

Production data is hosted in India (Supabase PostgreSQL ap-south-1; API on Render at https://houss-api.onrender.com). Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

7. How long we keep data

We keep personal data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. Voice audio is not retained after transcription. A detailed schedule is in our Data Retention Policy (repo / on request at info@houss.co.in).

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise personal data within 60 days, except where we must keep certain records for legal, tax, or dispute reasons.

8. Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to access, correct, withdraw consent where applicable, request deletion, and raise a grievance.

Account deletion

In the app: Profile → Account & privacy → Delete my account (three confirmations). You are logged out immediately; we complete deletion within 60 days.

By email: info@houss.co.in with subject "Delete my Houss account" and your registered mobile number.

Main household owners must transfer ownership or remove the household first. Web instructions: host account-deletion.html at e.g. https://www.houss.co.in/account-deletion.

Notifications

You can disable push notifications in your phone's system settings. Some in-app alerts may still appear while you use Houss.

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures to protect data, including access controls, household isolation on the server, and encrypted connections in production. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; please use a phone you control and do not share OTP codes.

10. Children

Houss is not directed at children under 18 to sign up on their own. A household owner may add a child account with controlled permissions.

11. Third-party links

Our website or app may link to third-party services (for example opening a location in Google Maps). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version at www.houss.co.in and update the effective date. For material changes, we may also notify you in the app or by email.
